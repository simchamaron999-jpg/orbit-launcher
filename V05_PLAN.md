# Orbit Launcher 0.5 — Release Candidate Plan

## Sourced Wallpaper Categories (150 Total)
The built-in gallery is expanded to **150 portrait-native wallpapers** (25 per category) across the six requested categories:
1. **Landscapes**: Scenic mountains, forests, alpine lakes, canyons, and waterfalls.
2. **Dogs**: Expressive portraits of various dog breeds in natural light.
3. **Animals**: Wildlife, birds, marine life, and mammals in portrait composition.
4. **Foods**: Fresh ingredients, artisan dishes, coffee, and vibrant culinary photography.
5. **Flowers**: Macro and field photography of roses, tulips, wildflowers, and blooms.
6. **Objects**: Minimalist everyday object photography, geometry, textures, and still life.

## Navigation & Usability Changes
- **Top Category Bar**: Settings replaces collapsible lists with horizontal chip buttons at the top (Quick, Gestures, Apps, Look, Wallpapers, Widgets, System) to jump directly to sections.
- **Back/Home Handling**: Pressing the device Back button or navigating away properly dismisses the settings sheet and returns directly to the active home launcher surface.
- **Divider-Free Design**: All harsh borders remain removed, relying entirely on Material 3 tonal surfaces and spacing.

## New Release-Ready Features
1. **Home Presets**: One-tap layouts (Minimalist 8-app, Balanced 12-app, Dense 16-app) that configure circle density and scale instantly.
2. **Layout Backup & Restore**: Export and import launcher preferences, favourites, and gesture mappings as a local JSON file.
